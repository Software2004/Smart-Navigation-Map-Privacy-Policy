Waypoint 01 / 09

Introduction

Smart GPS Navigator ("the app," "we," "us") provides turn‑by‑turn navigation, traffic information, parking‑spot saving, time‑stamped photos, a fuel calculator, a speedometer, and a compass. This Privacy Policy describes the information the app collects, how it's used, who it's shared with, and the controls available to you.

This policy applies to the app as distributed on Google Play and is effective as of 30 June 2026. By installing or using Smart GPS Navigator, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here.

Waypoint 02 / 09

Information we collect

The app is built around location and a few device features. Here's exactly what falls into each category.

Location

Precise (GPS) and approximate location, collected while navigating, viewing traffic, or saving a parking spot. Powered by Mapbox and LocationIQ.

Camera & photos

Camera access to capture time‑stamped, location‑tagged photos. Images are saved to your device, not uploaded by the app.

Device information

Device model, operating system version, and general network type (Wi‑Fi or mobile), used for compatibility and diagnostics.

Usage data

Which features you open — navigation, fuel calculator, speedometer, compass — to understand what's useful and what needs work.

Advertising data

Advertising ID and consent status, handled through Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent flow.

Sensor‑only features

The speedometer, compass, and fuel calculator read your device's GPS and motion sensors, or numbers you type in, entirely on‑device. None of that is transmitted anywhere by the app.

Waypoint 03 / 09

How we use information

  • Navigation and traffic — calculating routes, estimating arrival times, and showing live traffic conditions.
  • Parking spots — remembering where you parked so you can find your way back.
  • App improvement — understanding which features are used, and fixing what isn't working.
  • Advertising — showing ads through AdMob, only after you've made a consent choice via the UMP prompt.
  • Preferences — remembering your unit settings, map style, and other in‑app choices.

Waypoint 04 / 09

Data sharing

We don't sell your information. A small number of service providers process data on our behalf, strictly to run the features below.

Mapbox

Renders maps and calculates routes using your location data.

LocationIQ

Converts coordinates into addresses and place names (geocoding).

Google AdMob

Serves banner, interstitial, native, rewarded, and app‑open ads, governed by your consent choice.

Google Play Services

Supplies underlying location and device APIs the app runs on.

Each provider only receives the data needed to perform its function, and is bound by its own privacy policy and our usage terms. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Waypoint 05 / 09

Data storage

Saved parking spots and time‑stamped photos are stored locally on your device using a Room database — they are not copied to our servers or any cloud service unless this policy is updated to state otherwise.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your device settings, permanently deletes this locally stored data.

Waypoint 06 / 09

Your rights

Depending on where you live — including under GDPR in the EU/UK and CCPA in California — you may have additional rights. In all cases, the app gives you direct, practical control:

Review the location and parking data saved on your device, in‑app.

Delete saved parking spots and photos at any time from within the app.

Opt out of personalized ads through the UMP consent prompt or your device's ad settings.

Revoke location permission any time from your device's app settings.

Request access or deletion of any other data we hold, by contacting us directly.

Waypoint 07 / 09

Children's privacy

Smart GPS Navigator is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we'll remove it promptly.

Waypoint 08 / 09

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app's features evolve. Material changes will be reflected by a new effective date at the top of this page, and — where appropriate — an in‑app notice. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
